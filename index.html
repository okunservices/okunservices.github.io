Always professional, courteous and on time. Okun Services is a Great Barrington based gutter and window cleaning company founded by local resident Ted Okun.

Our small team serves over 1,500 customers in the southern Berkshire area. From locals who have lived here their entire lives, newcomers to the area and contractors needing to move their projects along.

Gutter Cleaning

Your gutters and downspouts will be free and clear of all debris. The debris removed will either be brought to a wooded location on your property or hauled away off site. We use different cleaning methods depending on the situation; most commonly by hand, although occasionally a leaf blower is best.

We guarantee your property to be just as tidy as it was when we arrived.

*We do not offer repairs or installation.

Window Cleaning

Interior and exterior window cleaning with the highest attention to detail. From the classic squeegee to embracing the newer technology of the water fed pole. Your windows will be crystal clear and streak free guaranteed.

Everyone knows what a squeegee is, but some may not be familiar with the water fed pole. To learn more, read on...

In the photo below you will see a 3-stage water filtration system. This machine filters all the minerals from your tap water creating completely pure water. The water travels through the telescoping pole and runs through a soft bristled brush which gently scrubs the glass. When it dries, your windows will be completely spotless. Magical!
